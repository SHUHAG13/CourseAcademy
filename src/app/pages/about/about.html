About Course Academy

A skill development platform from Bangladesh, built around one idea: learning should lead to real work.

Who we are

Practical skills for creative and tech careers

Course Academy is an online learning platform where students start as beginners and grow into professionals. We teach three career tracks — Video Editing, Sports Journalism and Software Development — through video courses taught in Bangla by people who do this work every day.

Beyond courses, we're building an ecosystem: our own sports media channel and software projects give skilled students a place to gain experience, earn and grow.

Our mission

To empower students in Bangladesh with practical digital skills — video editing, sports journalism and software development — so they can build creative, professional and well-paid careers.

Our vision

To become a leading skill development ecosystem in Bangladesh, producing editors, journalists and developers who can work with teams anywhere in the world.
